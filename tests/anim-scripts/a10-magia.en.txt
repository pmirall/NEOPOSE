EXT. FOREST - NIGHT

LYRA, a young witch with a staff, faces GORN, a huge orc warrior with an axe.

GORN
Your magic is weak!

LYRA
Let's see.

Lyra raises her staff and casts a fireball. Gorn blocks it with his axe. An explosion lights the forest.

Gorn charges at Lyra. Lyra jumps back and casts lightning. Gorn falls.

LYRA
(breathing hard)
Not that weak.
