INT. KITCHEN - MORNING

BOB, a clumsy chubby cook in an apron, carries a cake. SUE, his boss, watches with crossed arms.

SUE
Careful with that.

BOB
Relax, I've got it!

Bob trips and falls. The cake flies. Sue screams.

Bob gets up, looks at the floor and shrugs.

BOB
Five second rule?

Sue facepalms. Bob laughs.
