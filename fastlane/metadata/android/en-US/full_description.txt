<p>NEOPOSE is a 3D mannequin for artists. Pose one or more characters, dress them, place them in a lit set and use the result as a drawing reference. Everything works offline except the optional avatar gallery.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Posing</b>: IK (drag hands, feet and hips), FK (drag any joint and only its bone turns) or rotate each joint. Pin hands and feet in place, blend two poses, seat characters on chairs, sofas or beds. Almost 100 poses for one to four characters, hand gestures, gaze and facial expressions.</li>
<li><b>Pose from a photo</b>: the pose of a person in a picture, found on the device itself (MediaPipe BlazePose), with the photo kept over the canvas as a reference.</li>
<li><b>Body</b>: human anatomy from MakeHuman from childhood to old age, with gender, muscle, weight, height and stylisation, from realistic to chibi. Ten hairstyles in any colour, and a real skeleton view.</li>
<li><b>Anthro</b>: wolf, fox, dog, cat, rabbit, goat and dragon with sculpted heads, digitigrade or hoofed legs, claws and tails.</li>
<li><b>Avatars</b>: import your own humanoid VRM, GLB or FBX, or pick a free one from the Open Source Avatars gallery (needs Internet).</li>
<li><b>Clothes</b>: 469 garments that fit any body and pose.</li>
<li><b>Sets</b>: 24 places with their own lamps and shadows, 8 360° panoramas, and real objects to place.</li>
<li><b>Camera</b>: lens, Dutch angle, one-, two- and three-point perspective framings with vanishing guides, and a four-view layout.</li>
<li><b>Muscles that react to the pose</b>, with dual quaternion skinning; clothes and avatars follow.</li>
<li><b>Every kind of body</b>: builds from slim to plus size and pregnancy, proportions, muscle and fat, limb differences with prostheses.</li>
<li><b>Animation</b>: films of shots with their own cast, set and camera; keys, onion skin, animation principles (easing, anticipation, bounce, drag, arcs, squash and stretch); a gallery of 96 moves plus BVH/GLB/FBX import; storyboards, photos or video to animation; lip-synced script and subtitles. Export PNG frames, frames by layer, storyboard, MP4/WebM, GIF or BVH.</li>
<li><b>From script to film</b>: write a scene (Fountain or plain "ANNA: Hi!") and it animates itself, no AI: cast, set and time of day, 70+ actions with synonyms in seven languages, on-device voices with lip sync, subtitles and film-grammar camera shots.</li>
<li><b>Free references</b>: search openly licensed photos, GIFs and panoramas (Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, Poly Haven) and turn them into poses, animations and sets, with an on-device content filter.</li>
<li><b>For your drawing app</b>: layered PSD (set, each character, lines, sketch and an empty layer to draw on), shared straight to Krita, ibisPaint, Clip Studio…</li>
<li><b>My characters</b>: save a whole character (body, clothes, prostheses) and put them in any scene again, or share them as a file.</li>
<li><b>Mobility</b>: poses and animations with crutches, a walking stick, a walking frame and a wheelchair.</li>
<li><b>Export</b>: PNG up to A3 at 300 ppi, turnaround sheets, AI maps (depth, normals, OpenPose, lines), posed GLB mesh and BVH.</li>
<li><b>Drawing</b>: construction, volume, pencil, values and silhouette finishes, guides and a sketch layer; line of action, balance, exaggeration and squash and stretch for still pictures too. Gesture practice with a timer, classes and random bodies, clothes and sets.</li>
<li>In English and Spanish, plus Catalan, French, Portuguese, German and Italian (machine translations awaiting review). Scenes can be shared as a link.</li>
</ul>
